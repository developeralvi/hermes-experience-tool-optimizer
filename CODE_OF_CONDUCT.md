# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in EBTTO a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or dismissive remarks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated. Consequences may include a warning, temporary ban, or
permanent exclusion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all EBTTO spaces, including GitHub
issues, pull requests, and discussions.
